JST BOOST for Young Researchers

Care technologies that work together around people.

Development of a Multi-Agent Collaborative Care Support System

JPMJBY25C5 · Selected in FY2025
Principal investigator: Xun Shen

We study how care robots, exoskeletons, and AI wheelchairs can coordinate around the intentions and conditions of caregivers and care recipients. Combining human understanding with safe learning and control, the project aims to reduce caregiver burden and support independence and quality of life.

Research host: Institute of Science Tokyo through a cross-appointment, in coordination with the lab's research at TUAT.

Official project information↗ (external link)

What we aim to achieve

  1. Understand care situations through vision and sound
  2. Coordinate assistance with human-aware safety constraints
  3. Evaluate burden, acceptability, and quality of life
JSPS KAKENHI Scientific Research (B)

Connect learning with support throughout driving life.

Lifelong Support for Older Driver Education through Symbiotic Mobility Digital Twins

26K00525 · April 2026–March 2029
Principal investigator: Xun Shen

A mobility digital twin combines VR and generative AI to visualize how driving situations may unfold. We develop learning methods that adapt with limited data, connecting continued education with personalized driving support as an older driver's characteristics and needs change.

Host: TUAT. Co-investigators: Katsuki Fujisawa (Institute of Science Tokyo) and Masaki Inoue (Keio University).

Official project information↗ (external link)

What we aim to achieve

  1. Predict and visualize evolving driving situations
  2. Learn efficiently from knowledge and limited data
  3. Connect continued education with everyday driving support
JST Moonshot Goal 2

Understand organ networks to detect early signs of disease.

Comprehensive Mathematical Understanding of the Complex Control System between Organs and Challenge for Ultra-Early Precision Medicine

JPMJMS2021 · Ongoing
Research participant: Xun Shen (PI Jun-ichi Imura’s group)

We participate in Goal 2, which aims for ultra-early disease prediction and prevention by 2050. Within PM Kazuyuki Aihara’s project, Xun Shen works in PI Jun-ichi Imura’s group on mathematical, observation, and control approaches to understanding organ networks.

Project Manager (PM): Kazuyuki Aihara. Group Principal Investigator (PI): Jun-ichi Imura (Institute of Science Tokyo).

Official project information↗ (external link)Goal 2 (Cabinet Office)↗ (external link)

Group research focus

  1. Observe and estimate interactions between organs
  2. Model changes in health states mathematically
  3. Develop control theory for ultra-early intervention
