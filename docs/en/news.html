2026.11.16Award

Komori and Katayama’s team wins overall Second Place at the APAC HPC-AI Competition

Tokyo Rapids w/ RIKEN, including Shen Laboratory’s third-year undergraduates 小森悠太 (Komori) and 片山朋和 (Katayama), won overall Second Place at the 9th APAC HPC-AI Competition. The organizers announced the results at SC26 on November 16, 2026.

The team brings together students from Tokyo University of Science, Tokyo University of Agriculture and Technology, and Shibaura Institute of Technology. Two teams received the overall Second Place award.

The competition brought together 48 teams from 13 Asia-Pacific regions, with 33 reaching the final stage. Participants included students from world-leading universities such as the National University of Singapore (NUS) and Nanyang Technological University (NTU).

The challenges involved accelerating OpenFOAM fluid simulations and improving Qwen3 large language model inference performance under strict latency constraints. Congratulations to Komori, Katayama and the entire team!
