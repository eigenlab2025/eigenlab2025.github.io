A few practical questions

Do I need a fully developed research proposal?

A clear interest and a willingness to learn are a good starting point. We can discuss how to turn an interest into a research question.

What preparation is useful?

Linear algebra, probability, optimization, and programming are useful foundations. The appropriate preparation depends on the research topic, and students continue building skills after joining.

What does research supervision look like?

Faculty and senior students support discussions about papers, implementations, experimental design, and interpretation of results. Research questions and directions are developed together.

Are projects purely theoretical?

We connect theory with algorithms and evaluation. Practical questions help us identify which assumptions matter and where new methods are needed.
