OUR APPROACH

How research takes shape.

Students do not need to arrive with a finished research idea. We develop the question together.

01

Build foundations

Learn the mathematics, machine learning, and programming needed for your question.

02

Reproduce a method

Read a paper, understand its code, and run the experiments.

03

Find the gap

Identify when a method succeeds, where it fails, and why.

04

Improve & evaluate

Implement an idea, compare fairly, and communicate the evidence.
